# Simple Design System

## Personality

Warm, grounded, private, and practical. The site should feel trustworthy and calm without becoming decorative or vague.

## Colors

- Ink: `#24312d` for primary text and footer.
- Muted: `#5f6d68` for supporting copy.
- Paper: `#fffdf8` for the main page background.
- Surface: `#f7f0e4` for quiet content bands.
- Sage: `#496a5d` and `#7f9a8b` for trust, buttons, and calm emphasis.
- Rose: `#b56f6a` for section labels and human warmth.
- Amber: `#d9a441` for focus states and gentle highlights.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia for large, human headlines.
- Body: system sans-serif for readable, low-maintenance UI text.
- Letter spacing stays at `0`.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: `1160px`.
- Cards use `8px` radius.
- Full-width muted bands are used for major sections.
- Repeated items may use cards; page sections should stay unframed.

## Components

- Primary button: sage background, white text.
- Secondary button: white background, line border.
- Trust chips: short, concrete reassurance.
- Cards: used only for repeated concerns, session options, and the booking form.

## Content Rules

- Avoid clinical promises, diagnosis language, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Present astrology as optional reflection only.
- Keep booking language private, simple, and low-pressure.
- Use real credentials, testimonials, and photos only when Rajashri approves them.
