Where you may feel stuck

You might recognise yourself here.

You do not need a label or a perfectly explained problem. Start with the sentence that feels closest to what you are carrying.

Overthinking

My mind will not switch off.

I replay conversations, imagine what could go wrong, or feel tired from thinking about everything.

Carrying too much

Everyone depends on me.

I keep things running for others, but feel unseen, unsupported, or close to exhaustion.

Boundaries

I know what I need, but I struggle to say it.

I agree when I want to say no, avoid difficult conversations, or feel guilty for choosing myself.

Relationships

A relationship is leaving me confused or hurt.

I am unsure what to say, what to accept, or how to move forward.

Family pressure

Other people's expectations are shaping my life.

I feel torn between keeping the peace and making choices that feel right for me.

Change and identity

I do not feel like myself anymore.

Marriage, motherhood, career change, separation, an empty nest, or starting again has left me feeling lost.

None of these need to fit exactly. You can also begin simply because you want to understand yourself better.

How sessions work

A seven-step way to move from confusion to action.

Begin with whatever feels most present. Not every conversation needs every step; this is the broad path Rajashri uses to help you understand what is happening and find a practical way forward.

  1. 01 - Clarity Put words to what feels difficult and name the issue you want to understand.
  2. 02 - Notice the stuck place Recognise where you feel stuck, what keeps repeating, and what you have already tried.
  3. 03 - Find the cause Explore the thoughts, feelings, relationships, and circumstances beneath the issue.
  4. 04 - Steady the mind Use simple reflection and practical exercises to create more calm and choice.
  5. 05 - Define your goals Get clear about what you want to change, protect, begin, or move towards.
  6. 06 - Take practical steps Choose realistic actions, boundaries, habits, or conversations that fit your life.
  7. 07 - Maintain and grow Keep what helps through journaling, gratitude, affirmations, strengths-based reflection, movement, nutrition, and optional astrology reflection.

Knowing the scope

The right support for the right moment.

Coaching can help with everyday challenges and decisions. It does not replace clinical care or emergency support.

Coaching can help with

  • Understanding what you are feeling
  • Seeing repeated patterns in relationships or family life
  • Finding language for difficult conversations
  • Making decisions with more calm and confidence
  • Choosing small actions that fit your real life

Coaching is not

  • Emergency or crisis support
  • A replacement for therapy or psychiatric care
  • A place for medical diagnosis or treatment
  • A promise that one session will fix everything
  • A decision made on your behalf

Common questions

You may be wondering...

Is my problem big enough for coaching?

Yes. You can come even if nothing dramatic has happened. Coaching can help when you feel confused, tired, stuck, or unsure what to do next.

Will I be judged or told what to do?

No. The session is a private space to be heard clearly. Rajashri may help you see patterns and options, but the final choice stays with you.

What if I cry or cannot explain everything properly?

That is completely okay. You do not have to arrive with perfect words. The conversation can begin from wherever you are.

Can I talk about marriage, family, or personal matters privately?

Yes. You can bring sensitive topics at your own pace. The aim is to help you feel calmer, clearer, and less alone with what you are carrying.

Is astrology part of every session?

No. Life coaching is conversation-led and focused on your real life. If astrology is meaningful to you, it can be included as an optional reflective lens.
