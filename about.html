Why clients come

Most women do not arrive with one neat problem.

They come because they are tired of carrying too much quietly. They may be managing family expectations, relationship confusion, loneliness, anger, guilt, overthinking, or a decision that feels difficult to make alone.

Rajashri's role is not to judge, label, or take over your life. Her role is to listen carefully, help you name what is happening, and support you in choosing one grounded next step.

How it feels

A session should feel private, respectful, and useful.

You do not have to perform strength. You do not have to explain everything perfectly. You can begin from the sentence that feels most true today.

You are heard first

Rajashri starts by understanding your situation in your words, without rushing into advice.

Your emotions are not dismissed

If something feels painful, confusing, or heavy, it is treated with care rather than minimized.

Your choices remain yours

The session can help you see options more clearly, but it does not pressure you into one answer.

You leave with something practical

That may be a reflection prompt, a conversation to try, a boundary to practice, or one next step.

Clear boundaries

What this support is, and what it is not.

Clear boundaries are part of trust. Coaching can be deeply supportive, but it should not pretend to be everything.

Coaching can help with

  • Understanding what you are feeling
  • Seeing repeated patterns in relationships or family life
  • Finding language for difficult conversations
  • Making decisions with more calm and confidence
  • Choosing small actions that fit your real life

Coaching is not

  • Emergency or crisis support
  • A replacement for therapy or psychiatric care
  • A place for medical diagnosis or treatment
  • A promise that one session will fix everything
  • A decision made on your behalf

About astrology

Astrology is optional, never forced.

Some clients find astrology meaningful. Others prefer a purely conversation-led session. Both are welcome.

If you choose to include astrology, Rajashri can use it as a reflective lens for self-understanding, timing, and patterns. It is not used to frighten you, remove your agency, or replace practical action.

Before you enquire

Questions that help you decide if this feels right.

Can I speak in my own language and pace?

Yes. The session should move at a pace that feels safe enough for you to speak honestly.

Do I need to know exactly what I want from the session?

No. Many people begin with confusion. The first part of the conversation can simply be finding the words.

Will I be pushed to take a big decision immediately?

No. A good next step may be small: naming a feeling, preparing a conversation, resting, or noticing a pattern.

Can I ask about fees and timing before booking?

Yes. The enquiry is private and low-pressure. You can ask about availability, session format, and fees first.
